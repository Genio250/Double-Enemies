A mod that doubles all enemies in the game.  
  
This is the full release version. It doesn't support everything yet, but it has most features that I want working.  
Modded bosses and enemies will get duped, but them working properly, if at all, is not guaranteed, so be careful.  
  
Features:  
    * Double Enemies: Almost all enemies in the game are doubled.  
    * Synced HP: All supported bosses will have their HP doubled and synchronized, also applies to some mini bosses.  
    * Enemies that would stick completely together are split appart.  
    * Mod Toggle: Toggles the mod.  
    * Only Bosses: Only duplicates bosses, currently not compatible with the colosseum.  
    * Colosseum Waves: Fixes colosseum waves so they won't be skipping with the double killing amounts. Can be disabled with Crazy Colo.  
    * Extra duplications: Currently can duplicate grimmkin flames and mawlek's mask shard with the Duplicate Drops setting. Also can duplicate lifeblood from cocoons with the Duplicate Lifeblood setting. Pale Lurker's key is always duped currently.  
  
Dependencies:  
    * Satchel  
    * Doubled Radiance: It is not needed for the code to run but Radiance and AbsRad are not supported natively.  
    * Invinsibiliy Monitor: It is not needed for the code to run, but bosses that scream mid battle like THK or Dung Defender can cause unavoidable hits.  
  
If you find any enemy not listed that is broken or doesn't get duped, let me know.  
  
Supported Mods:  
    * Everwatchers: All 4 difficulties. Toggle and HP share not supported.  
    * Dryya: She is the only boss from Pale Curt that works. Her staggers are synchronized but take 24 hits instead of 12. Text is glitched for some reason.  
    * Cagney Carnation: Boss and his ads work. Second phase remains at the vanilla hp because I decided.  
    * Pale Prince: I tested it and it worked fine lol, but I didn't touch it.  
    * Most Radiance mods, as supported by Double Radiance.  

Known issues:  
    * Most sources of damage that are not the nail or spells offset the hp sync and can make bosses invinsible (Grimmchild and Flukenest are confirmed to be broken).  
    * False Knight doesn't double its hp.  
    * Mantis Lords and SoB phase 2 duplicates are phase 1 mantis lords, so they don't do coordinated attacks.   
    * The Overworld Uumuu copy can't be popped by Quirell and runs out of attacks.  
    * Some enemies just cannot be duplicated and will have to be checked manually.    
    * Soul Warrior in his ascended arena and in the colosseum sometimes teleports out of bounds.  
    * Soul Warrior 1 in the overworld has no AI when entering the arena from the breakable roof.  
    * The follies wave in Colo 3 is not duplicated.  
    * Crystal and Enraged Guardian in the overworld still break so they are disabled.
